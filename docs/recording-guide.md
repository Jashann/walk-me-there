# Recording someone describing a home

Walk Me There builds from how people really talk, in their own language. A phone voice memo is enough.

## Setup
- A quiet room. Put the phone on the table between you, screen down. Close windows and turn off the TV and fans.
- Start with a minute or two. The tested recordings run 23 to 90 seconds; longer ones should work, because the app splits them, but they have not been measured and cost more.
- Let them speak in whatever language they like, mixed with English or not.
- You ask, they answer. Do not correct them. If they change their mind ("no, the kitchen was on the other side"), let them; the app keeps the corrected version.
- The questions that help most are about **where things were as you walk in**: left, right, at the back, next to what.

## Questions (Punjabi and English)

1. ਘਰ ਕਿਹੜੇ ਪਿੰਡ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੀ? ਘਰ ਕਿਹੋ ਜਿਹਾ ਸੀ, ਕੱਚਾ ਕਿ ਪੱਕਾ?
   Which village was the house in? Was it mud brick or fired brick?
2. ਬਾਹਰੋਂ ਅੰਦਰ ਵੜਦਿਆਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਕੀ ਆਉਂਦਾ ਸੀ? ਦਰਵਾਜ਼ਾ ਕਿਹੋ ਜਿਹਾ ਸੀ?
   Coming in from outside, what came first? What was the gate or door like?
3. ਅੰਦਰ ਆ ਕੇ ਖੱਬੇ ਹੱਥ ਕੀ ਸੀ? ਸੱਜੇ ਹੱਥ ਕੀ ਸੀ?
   Once you were inside, what was on your left? What was on your right?
4. ਵਿਹੜਾ ਕਿੰਨਾ ਕੁ ਵੱਡਾ ਸੀ? ਵਿਹੜੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੀ ਕੀ ਸੀ? ਨਲਕਾ, ਚੁੱਲ੍ਹਾ, ਕੋਈ ਦਰੱਖ਼ਤ?
   How big was the courtyard? What was in it? A hand pump, a stove, a tree?
5. ਪਿੱਛੇ ਕਿਹੜੇ ਕਮਰੇ ਸਨ? ਕਿਹੜਾ ਕਮਰਾ ਕਿਸ ਦਾ ਸੀ?
   Which rooms were at the back? Whose room was which?
6. ਬੇਬੇ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਸੌਂਦੇ ਸੀ? ਉਹਨਾਂ ਦੀ ਮੰਜੀ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਸੀ?
   Where did Bebe sleep? Where was her manji?
7. ਰੋਟੀ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਪੱਕਦੀ ਸੀ? ਚੌਂਕਾ ਜਾਂ ਰਸੋਈ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਸੀ?
   Where was the cooking done? Where was the chaunka or kitchen?
8. ਛੱਤ ਉੱਤੇ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਸੀ? ਪੌੜੀਆਂ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਸਨ?
   How did you get up on the roof? Where were the stairs?
9. ਪਸ਼ੂ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਬੰਨ੍ਹਦੇ ਸੀ? ਤੂੜੀ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਰੱਖਦੇ ਸੀ?
   Where were the animals tied? Where was the straw kept?
10. ਕੋਈ ਚੀਜ਼ ਜਿਹੜੀ ਉਸ ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੀ ਤੇ ਹੋਰ ਕਿਤੇ ਨਹੀਂ ਦੇਖੀ?
    Something in that house you never saw anywhere else?

Follow-ups that help: "ਵੜਦਿਆਂ ਖੱਬੇ ਕਿ ਸੱਜੇ?" (left or right as you walk in?), "ਕਿਸ ਦੇ ਨਾਲ?" (next to what?), "ਕਿੰਨਾ ਕੁ ਵੱਡਾ, ਮੰਜੀ ਜਿੱਡਾ?" (how big, the size of a cot?).

## After the first build
Open the house and look at **Ask next**. Ask those questions in the next recording, then upload it to the same house (or a new one). Each answer fills a grey guess with something that was actually said.

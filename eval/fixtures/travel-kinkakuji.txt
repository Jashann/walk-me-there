We went to Kyoto in November, and the one place I still think about is Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion.
You walk in from the gate along a gravel path under the trees, and then it opens up and there is the pond in front of you, very wide and very still.
And on the far side of the pond, right at the water's edge, is the pavilion, three floors, and the top two floors are completely covered in gold.
The bottom floor is plain dark wood and white, and then the gold starts above it, and on the very top of the roof there is a little golden phoenix.
The whole thing is reflected upside down in the water, perfectly, gold in the water. That is the picture everyone takes, and we took it too.
In the pond there are small islands with rocks and little pine trees on them, twisted ones, the way they cut pines in Japan.
All around the pond the maple trees were bright red and orange, it was the right week for the leaves, and behind the pavilion the hill is covered in trees.
The path goes along the left side of the pond, and there are stone lanterns along it.
There was a small waterfall somewhere behind, and a little tea house up the hill, but I don't remember exactly where.
It was crowded but everyone was quiet, just looking at it.

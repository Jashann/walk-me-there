We went to the Taj Mahal in 1998, the whole family, it was winter and there was a little fog in the morning.
You come in through a big red gate, red stone with white marble patterns, and the gate has small white domes on top of it.
When you come through the arch of the gate, you see it, the Taj, far away at the other end of the garden, all white.
In between there is a long water channel going straight to it, with fountains in the middle, and trees on both sides, cypress trees in rows.
Halfway there is a raised marble pool where everyone takes the photo, the bench where Princess Diana sat, my daughter wanted the same photo.
The Taj stands on a big white platform, and you have to take your shoes off or put covers on before you go up the steps.
There are four tall minarets, one on each corner of the platform, and they lean a little outward, the guide said so they don't fall on the tomb.
The big dome on top is like an onion, very big, and there are four small domes around it.
On the left side of the Taj there is a red mosque with three white domes, and on the right side the same building again, the guide called it the jawab.
Behind the Taj is the river, the Yamuna, we could see it from the platform.

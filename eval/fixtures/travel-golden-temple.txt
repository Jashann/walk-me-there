The first time I went to Darbar Sahib I was maybe twelve, we went with my mother and my uncle on the bus from the village.
We came in through the big gate with the clock tower, the one on the north side, and you go down some steps and suddenly there is the whole sarovar in front of you.
The water was so big, and right in the middle was Harmandir Sahib, all gold, shining in the sun.
The floor around the water was white marble and it was so hot on our feet in the afternoon that we walked on the wet mats.
To go to Harmandir Sahib there is a long walkway, a bridge over the water, with gold lamps on both sides, and we stood in the line on that bridge for almost an hour.
The bottom of Harmandir Sahib is white marble and the top part is all gold, with a gold dome on top and small domes on the corners.
On the left side as you come down the steps, near the water, there was a big old tree, the ber tree, and people were sitting under it.
Across the water, on the far side, there was Akal Takht, a tall building facing Harmandir Sahib.
And the two tall flags, the Nishan Sahibs, were standing near Akal Takht, orange.
After that we went to the langar hall on the right side and sat on the floor in long lines and ate.
